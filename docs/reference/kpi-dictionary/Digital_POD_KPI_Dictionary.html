Executive Scorecard KPIs

Top-line metrics surfaced directly to leadership for strategic decision-making

Purpose: These 8 KPIs represent the clearest signal of Digital's impact on business outcomes — owner engagement, feature ROI, and arrival-driving efficiency. Each is designed to translate digital activity into business language.
01 · Outcome
Total Arrivals
Completed stays across all booking channels. Represents the primary business outcome — a guest physically arriving and checking in.
Source: RES_FACT
02 · Attribution
Digital Arrivals Share
Percentage of all arrivals that originated from a Web or App booking. Measures the share of business that digital channels are driving.
Source: RES_FACT + GA4
03 · Engagement
Arrivals per Owner (APO)
Average number of completed stays per unique owner. Normalizes arrival volume against owner base size for fair period-over-period comparison.
Source: RES_FACT + Membership
04 · Engagement
Bookings per Owner (BPO)
Average number of reservations placed per unique owner. A leading indicator of owner intent and platform engagement before stays are completed.
Source: RES_FACT + Membership
05 · Feature Value
Lift vs Control
Incremental improvement in a KPI (arrivals, bookings) driven by a specific feature, measured against a comparable control group.
Source: Experiment / Model
06 · Feature Value
Incremental Arrivals (YTD)
Estimated additional arrivals attributable to feature releases year-to-date. Translates engineering output into tangible business outcomes.
Source: Model Output
07 · ROI
Feature ROI
Financial return generated from feature-driven incremental outcomes. Calculated as (Incremental Revenue − Cost) / Cost. The core investment justification metric.
Source: Finance + Model
08 · Efficiency
Search-to-Arrival Ratio
Number of search sessions required to produce one arrival. A lower ratio indicates a more efficient path from discovery to booking to stay.
Source: GA4 + RES_FACT

CW & WM App KPIs

Shared metric definitions for the Club Wyndham and WorldMark mobile applications, including GA4 inflation adjustment logic

GA4 Inflation Note: Several metrics use an inflation-adjusted fallback when real-time GA4 data is unavailable. When "Estimated (Inflated) Metrics" are used, they are multiplied by a correction factor (typically 1.71 for session-type metrics, 1.975 for checkout, 2.169 for searches) to approximate real-time values. The formula pattern is: IF(actual > 0, actual, ROUND(estimated × factor, 0)).
